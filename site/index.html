INDIVIDUAL

AMC 10 / 12

American Mathematics Competitions

The AMC 10 and AMC 12 are 25-question, 75-minute individual contests. Strong scores can lead to an invitation to the AIME.

Focus: breadth, accuracy, and time management.
Official guide ↗
INDIVIDUAL & TEAM

BMT

Berkeley Math Tournament

A UC Berkeley student-led tournament with Individual, Power, and Guts rounds. Preparation includes unfamiliar problems, proof writing, and collaboration.

Focus: proof writing and team problem solving.
Official archive ↗Official site ↗
INDIVIDUAL & TEAM

SMT

Stanford Math Tournament

A Stanford student-run tournament with individual and team rounds. The archives include subject tests, team questions, and proof-based Power rounds.

Focus: depth and mathematical communication.
Official archive ↗Official site ↗

AMC 10 covers mathematics through grade 10; AMC 12 covers high school mathematics, including trigonometry, without calculus. Check the official sites for full eligibility, current formats, dates, and registration. Club preparation does not imply a confirmed school registration.

Sources & acknowledgmentsArchive and dataset information +

The library combines existing datasets with problems imported directly from the official BMT and SMT archives. Official entries have verified competition, year, round, and problem-number labels, original PDF artwork, and paired official solution links. No solutions are generated or inferred for those imports. Rounds without reliable numbering or necessary shared context are omitted. We deduplicate new imports against existing full statements. Cross-dataset duplicates may remain. A dataset label identifies its collection, not a verified original contest; contest names inferred from text are not used as official provenance.

  • Official BMT archive and official Stanford SMT archive — only paired original problems and solutions with verified numbering are imported.
  • AIME 1983–2024 — supplied exam metadata and answers; worked solutions are not included.
  • NuminaMath-CoT — AMC/AIME, olympiad, AoPS forum, and school-practice subsets. Some supplied solutions are machine-generated; school practice includes synthetic material.
  • MATH — competition-style problems and supplied solutions across seven subject categories.
  • OlympiadBench — English, text-only competition mathematics; original supplied solutions and answers retained.

Topics and difficulty bands are automatic estimates, not official contest ratings. Historical imports may have shortened statements or solutions and missing diagrams. BMT and SMT problems retain original mathematical notation and diagrams as images cropped from official papers. Extracted PDF text powers search and may have imperfect mathematical formatting; the original artwork is authoritative. Full original papers are linked from every official problem. All material remains credited to its respective authors and dataset publishers. Competition logos are used to identify their respective competitions; they do not imply affiliation or endorsement. Logo sources: MAA AMC, BMT, and SMT.
